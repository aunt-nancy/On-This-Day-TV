Priority & Execution Model

Operating rule: Automatic by default. No normal Start, Resume, Reset, or Run-All action is required from the administrator.
Core path: Opening Desk → Black Press + Major Press → Source Verification → Discrepancy Control → Regional/Local + Community Press → Context / Rights / Translation / Visual sourcing → Closing Desk → post-publish features → social distribution.
Approval isolation: A disputed source is held at the item level whenever it can be identified. Unrelated verified stories continue through the newsroom and can publish normally.
Publishing rule: Only verified source-backed stories may enter the public edition. OCR and machine translation accelerate discovery; they do not replace source verification or required human language review.
Automatic continuation: Approval or rejection queues the necessary downstream refresh automatically. There is no manual resume step after a decision.
