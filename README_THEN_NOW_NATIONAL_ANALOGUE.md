# On This Day — Then & Now National Analogue Parameters

APPROVED DEFINITION

NOW FIRST
The agent first identifies one important issue actively happening in the United States today/currently.

THEN SECOND
The historical comparison may come from ANY earlier period in United States history.

THEN IS NOT LIMITED TO
- 200 years ago
- 100 years ago
- 75 years ago
- the stories in today's main historical edition
- today's month/day
- anniversaries

MATCH STANDARD
The past and present must share at least two substantive features, such as:
- institution or branch of government
- law/policy mechanism
- economic or labor pressure
- civil-rights/social movement dynamics
- technology transition
- national emergency/public response
- immigration/housing/education/election/regulatory conflict
- comparable nationwide consequences

A superficial thematic resemblance is rejected.

SOURCES
At least one reliable current source and one reliable historical source are required.
Primary/government/institutional historical sources are preferred.

IF NO STRONG MATCH
Then & Now returns show=false rather than forcing a weak comparison.

EXECUTION
The existing bounded Then & Now execution limits remain unchanged.
The approved 19-agent bounded-parallel/nonblocking runner remains unchanged.
