# On This Day — National Headline / Same-Event Community Voices Fix

ROOT CAUSE
The prior edition builder independently chose the highest-confidence Major/other y100 story and the highest-confidence Black Press story. It did not require the two stories to share the same event and had no national-importance gate.

CORRECTED LOCKED EDITORIAL RULE
- Major American Press establishes ONE nationally important lead event.
- Black Press and every other Community Press Voice displayed as a juxtaposition must cover that SAME verified eventKey.
- Different framing, emphasis, criticism, omissions, community stakes, and point of view are encouraged.
- Different event/topic is prohibited from the lead comparison.
- Unrelated same-day community stories may remain secondary/archive content but cannot masquerade as a lens on the lead.
- If the most important national event lacks community coverage, the selector prefers another genuinely nationally important event with meaningful same-event community coverage.
- If no same-event community coverage is verified, the system does not fabricate a comparison.

EXECUTION
Editor Opening
→ Major American Press national lead discovery
→ Black Press + Regional + Community agents in parallel targeting those lead candidates
→ Source Verification normalizes exact same-event clusters and re-checks national importance
→ rolling publication
→ remaining editorial/production work

NATIONAL IMPORTANCE
Examples include federal government/President/Congress/Supreme Court, national election/policy, war/diplomacy, major national economic or labor developments, nationally significant disasters/crises, or events with demonstrable broad U.S. newspaper prominence/nationwide consequence.

A local marriage, local crime, municipal matter, local business/oil-field update, society item, or community meeting does not qualify merely because it is interesting.
