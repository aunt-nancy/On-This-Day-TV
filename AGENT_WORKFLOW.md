# On This Day — Launch Agent Workflow

## Core rule
Agents proceed automatically when independent verification checks agree. Human intervention is exception-based, not routine.

## Daily pipeline
1. Date & Story Queue calculates 200 / 100 / 75-year windows.
2. Major Press Research finds the strongest national/metropolitan leads.
3. Black & Community Press Research finds same-event coverage and parallel lead stories.
4. Regional / Local Research identifies geographically relevant coverage.
5. Verification + Discrepancy checks issue date, publication identity, page, OCR, quotations, translations, rights flags and event-level factual support.
6. If checks pass, Publishing + Social assembles website and six-platform social packages.
7. If material conflicts remain, mark HOLD and send only the discrepancy to human review.

## Social outputs
- YouTube: Shorts + optional long-form daily edition
- Facebook: Reel + image/link card
- Instagram: Reel + carousel
- TikTok: vertical short
- X: headline comparison + thread/link
- Threads: narrative comparison + link

## One-day MVP principle
Do not block launch on advanced APIs, deep archive filtering, or complex dashboards. Generate ready-to-post packages where direct publishing connections are unavailable.
