Newspaper Selection Order

Reach first, then longevity

The national account is chosen from exact-date major dailies after comparing documented contemporary circulation or reach, front-page prominence, corroboration across other leading papers, and national consequence. Community papers are ranked by documented circulation or geographic/subscriber reach and then longevity. The comparison searches each ranked paper for the exact event first, the same verified topic second, and uses an unrelated lead only as a clearly labeled source audit when no topical item can be verified.

Archive availability never proves that a title was the largest or oldest. When circulation evidence is incomplete or only a regional issue survives, the edition states that limitation.
