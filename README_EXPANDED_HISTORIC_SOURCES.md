# On This Day — Expanded Historic Newspaper Source Hierarchy

APPROVED SOURCE POLICY

The newsroom must not depend on a small list of modern newspaper archives.

EARLY-REPUBLIC / 1826-ERA SEARCHES
Deliberately search older established newspapers that were actually publishing in that period:
- National Intelligencer
- New-York Evening Post
- Connecticut Courant / Hartford Courant
- New-Hampshire Gazette
- Providence Gazette
- Newport Mercury
- Columbian Centinel
- Boston Commercial Gazette
- Richmond Enquirer
- Charleston Courier
- major Baltimore and Savannah papers of the period
- Niles’ Weekly Register for contemporaneous national context when useful

1951 / MID-20TH-CENTURY SEARCHES
Use national papers of record plus major credible regional papers:
- New York Times
- Washington Post
- Wall Street Journal
- Christian Science Monitor
- Baltimore Sun
- Philadelphia Inquirer
- Chicago Tribune
- Los Angeles Times
- Boston Globe
- San Francisco Chronicle
- Atlanta Constitution / Atlanta Journal
- St. Louis Post-Dispatch
- other strong metropolitan papers

UNIVERSITY / INSTITUTIONAL COLLECTIONS
The source hierarchy now explicitly includes:
- UC Riverside California Digital Newspaper Collection
- University of Georgia Georgia Historic Newspapers
- Penn State Pennsylvania Newspaper Archive
- University of Illinois digital newspaper collections
- Indiana University Hoosier State Chronicles
- University of Nebraska–Lincoln newspaper collections
- University of Utah digital newspapers
- University of Oregon historic newspapers
- University of Florida Digital Newspaper Library
- DigitalNC / university-state partnerships
- University of South Carolina newspaper program
- University of North Texas Portal to Texas History
- state libraries, historical societies, university libraries/press archives, and special collections

SEARCH BEHAVIOR
- failure at one archive is not failure of the era;
- agents must move horizontally across newspaper titles and vertically across institutional collections;
- original issue/page scans are preferred;
- side-era recovery records what newspapers and archives were actually searched;
- trivial filler cannot replace a stronger available national/broadly consequential story.

The approved 19-agent bounded-parallel/nonblocking runner is unchanged.
