About & Method

On This Day reconstructs the news agenda of a historical date and compares how national, regional and community newspapers framed American life.

How a daily edition is built

The system calculates the 200-, 100- and 75-year windows, searches major newspaper archives, builds event clusters, then compares the strongest surviving coverage. The 100-year desk receives the largest visual area and prioritizes a mainstream-versus-Black-press comparison when the sources support it.

Community Press Voices are not treated as decorative sidebars. The publication, community, language, city and holding archive are identified so readers can see exactly what was searched.

Exception-based human review

Agents continue automatically when verification checks agree. Human intervention is requested only when a discrepancy remains unresolved: conflicting facts, uncertain attribution, ambiguous translation, questionable authenticity, or unresolved rights status.

HOLD FOR REVIEW: two archives give different publication dates and the issue image is incomplete. Automated publishing stops until the conflict is resolved.

Social distribution

The same verified daily research package feeds platform-native versions for YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X and Threads. API approval delays do not block the site launch; ready-to-post packages can be published manually until direct connections are available.
